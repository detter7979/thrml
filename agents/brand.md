# thrml Brand Guidelines

## Voice
Clean, grounded, premium-accessible. Not spa-luxury, not gym-bro.
Nordic-meets-California. Warm, private, tactile.

> Private wellness, yours for the hour.

Tone shifts by phase:

- P1 (awareness): poetic, sensory, scene-setting
- P2 (consideration): practical, benefit-led, warm
- P3 (conversion): direct, urgent, social-proofed

## Photo Style - DO
- Cedar saunas with steam, natural diffused light
- Cold plunges with mist or condensation
- Pacific Northwest forest, fog, mountain backdrops
- Empty spaces with no people, so the viewer projects themselves in
- Hands, feet, towels: partial body, never full faces
- Warm amber plus cool blue contrast
- Architectural symmetry, framed doorways, single subject

## Photo Style - DON'T
- Stock spa imagery: cucumber slices, white robes
- Crowded gym or wellness center vibes
- Bright fluorescent lighting
- Faces, especially smiling
- Neon, ultra-modern surfaces
- Tropical or beach settings, which are off-brand for PNW

## Headline Frameworks
1. Possessive: "Your hour. Your ritual."
2. Empty-space FOMO: "An empty sauna in your neighborhood."
3. Benefit-first: "Recovery. Without the membership."
4. Question: "When did you last have an hour to yourself?"
5. Social proof: "★★★★★ 'Best ritual I've found.'"

## CTA Library

- Find Your Space

- Explore Spaces
- See What's Near You

## Logo Placement
Wordmark always cream (`#F7F3EE`) on dark backgrounds.

Top-right corner, small, never larger than 60px wide on a 1080 canvas.
