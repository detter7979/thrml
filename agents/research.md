# thrml Competitive Research

## Direct competitors
- Peerspace — peer-to-peer space rental (broader than wellness)
- Splacer — event space rental
- Restorative Sauna (Seattle) — direct local competitor
- Othership — NYC/Toronto contrast therapy
- Bathhouse Brooklyn — NYC bathhouse

## Adjacent competitors
- ClassPass — wellness/fitness aggregator
- Mindbody — booking infrastructure
- Hotworx — infrared sauna chain
- Soltreme — at-home sauna sales

## Research cadence
Weekly Monday agent run:
1. Pull Meta Ad Library for each competitor (active ads in last 7 days)
2. Screenshot landing pages
3. Track pricing pages for changes (diff against last week)
4. Summarize positioning shifts in agent-digest

## What to look for
- Hook angles being tested (compare to thrml's)
- Format mix (video vs static, ratio)
- CTA language
- Social proof signals (review counts, testimonials)
- Promo cadence (any recurring discount patterns)
- New features mentioned in landing copy
